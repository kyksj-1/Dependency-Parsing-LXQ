\begin{abstract}
We report an empirical study of the Deep Biaffine dependency parser of \citet{dozat2017biaffine} on an 8.3K-sentence Chinese treebank. Fourteen training runs vary pretrained word vectors, optimizer choice, and contextual encoder. The best dev UAS across the matrix is 86.93, obtained by the BiLSTM trained with Muon for 100 epochs under warmup and cosine decay, slightly above the Adam baseline of 86.43. The largest Transformer reaches 83.92 UAS, which is below both BiLSTM configurations at the data scale we study. We document a post-norm-without-warmup failure mode that collapses the matched-budget Transformer to 12.20 UAS, and we find that Muon's Newton--Schulz step recovers four times the accuracy of Adam in that unstable regime. We release the training scripts, the Muon wrapper with auto-routing for embeddings and biases, and a preflight checklist consolidating the observed failure modes.
\end{abstract}
