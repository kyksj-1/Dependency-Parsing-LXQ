\section{Our Work}
\label{sec:our_work}

This section gives the matrix and the headline numbers before any technical content. Table~\ref{tab:matrix-overview} lists all fourteen runs that contribute to the analysis. The training corpus is the NLP\&CC 2013 Chinese dependency treebank with 8\,301 training sentences and 534 dev sentences (12\,987 evaluable arcs after punctuation filtering). Word vectors are the Chinese-Word-Vectors release of \citet{li2018cwv}. All runs share the same Biaffine head, MLP projections and MST decoder; only the encoder, the optimizer and the word vectors vary.

\paragraph{Headline numbers.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Best dev UAS = 86.93}, by BiLSTM + Muon + 100 epochs + warmup--cosine; $+0.50$ over the Adam baseline.
  \item \textbf{Largest Transformer = 83.92 UAS} (76M parameters, all modern tricks); $-2.51$ relative to the BiLSTM baseline.
  \item \textbf{SDPA-9M post-norm without warmup collapses} to 12.20 UAS (Adam) or 53.35 UAS (Muon); switching to pre-norm + warmup recovers to 81--82 UAS.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Variants we considered but did not run.} Mixture-of-Experts on the FFN, linear-attention substitutes, multi-head causal connections of \citet{deepseekteam2025mhc}, and the gated-attention variant of \citet{openreview2025gateattn}. Each targets a regime (much larger data, much longer sequences, or much larger pretraining) that does not match our 8.3K-sentence supervised treebank, and we omit them.
